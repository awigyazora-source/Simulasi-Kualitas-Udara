AQI Calculation Logic

The Air Quality Index is a piecewise linear function of the pollutant concentration. Formula:

Ip = [(Ihi-Ilo)/(BPhi-BPlo)] * (Cp-BPlo) + Ilo
Where Cp is the rounded concentration of pollutant p, BPhi/BPlo are the breakpoints, and Ihi/Ilo are the corresponding AQI values.
